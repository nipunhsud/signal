Under the hood

Four steps. Every 15 minutes. Market hours only.

Step 1 · Scan

Wide net, tight filter.

3,000+ stocks scanned every 15 minutes. Only patterns with tight consolidation, bullish MA stack, and confirmed volume expansion advance.

Step 2 · Classify

Actionable vs. Extension.

Every candidate is labeled Actionable (fresh breakout from a real base) or Extension (continuation of a recent breakout). Confidence weighted by consolidation quality, volume, and structure.

AI reads the earnings call.

Step 4 · Deliver

FAQ

Common questions.

Why not just use TradingView or TrendSpider?

Fair question. If you have the time to build multi-timeframe scanners with MA stacks + volume + consolidation filters, and then read every relevant earnings call yourself, TradingView Pro at $60/mo will get you close on the scanning side.

DataQuant is different in three ways:

  • Curation. You get 20-30 signals per month, not 500 alerts. Each pre-filtered to confidence > 80%.
  • AI earnings context on every signal. TradingView doesn't summarize earnings call tone, guidance, or risk flags — you'd read the transcript yourself.
  • Frozen entry & disciplined 8% stop. The trade setup is fixed at the moment of signal — no drift as prices move.

If your account is $50k+ and your time is worth more than $30/hour, the math flips. If not, TradingView is honestly a better choice.

30 days of data isn't a real backtest. Why should I trust these numbers?

You shouldn't — not as proof of a permanent edge. 30 days is a single market regime. Any breakout strategy will look good when the market is trending up.

What the 30-day sample proves is narrower: the system runs, the signals fire, the discipline holds. Treat it as evidence the mechanics work, not evidence they'll always work.

We publish updated results monthly — wins and losses both — so you can watch the sample grow across regimes. If the number drops in a chop or bear month, we'll say so. Try the free trial and evaluate on your own experience.

How does the AI earnings analysis work?

For every US-listed stock signal, we fetch the most recent earnings call transcript and run it through a large language model to extract: (1) overall tone score (bullish/neutral/bearish, -1 to +1), (2) guidance direction (raised/maintained/lowered), (3) 2-4 highlights pulled from management's own language, (4) 1-3 risk flags. This appears on the dashboard row and inside the signal detail drawer. Results are cached per quarter — the same analysis powers every downstream signal.
