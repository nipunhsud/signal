The product

One stock, four readings, one page.

Most tools show a chart, or a balance sheet, or a feed of posts. DataQuant puts the base, the business and the crowd side by side, and has AI say what they add up to.

Technicals

The base, graded.

Each base gets S, A+ or A from its length, its depth and where it sits against the 52-week high. Relative strength ranks the stock 1 to 99 against everything else scanned. A breakout is scored 1 to 10 from the factors that measured as a lift.

Questions

Asked and answered.

What is DataQuant?

DataQuant is a stock screener for US and Indian markets. It grades the base each liquid stock is building, reads the latest earnings call with AI, and tracks institutional and social activity around each name. It reports what the screen saw. It does not recommend trades.

How does DataQuant grade a breakout?

When a stock closes above the pivot of its base, the base is graded S, A+ or A by its length, depth and distance from the 52-week high.

The breakout is then scored 1 to 10 from factors that measured as a lift across 97,789 historical breakouts: relative strength, base grade, institutional activity, and how far and on how much volume the close cleared the pivot. Past 5% extension it loses points. The dashboard shows 7 and above by default.

What does the AI read?

A language model reads the most recent earnings call transcript for each US stock on the screen. It returns a tone score from −1 to +1, the direction of guidance, two to four highlights in management's own words and one to three risk flags. Results are cached per quarter.

What does popularity mean here?

Who is buying and who is talking. It combines an institutional activity score from 0 to 10 built from heavy-volume up and down days, the named funds on the 13F register and how much that register turned over, social sentiment from StockTwits and X, and the latest news.

Which stocks are covered?

About 3,300 US stocks and ETFs on NASDAQ, NYSE and AMEX above $300 million in market value and a liquidity floor, plus Indian NSE stocks. The US screen runs every 15 minutes during market hours and again after the close.

Can AI assistants and agents use DataQuant?

Yes. The MCP server at dataquant.ai/mcp serves the market-health gauge, sector strength, the base X-ray for any stock and search over the research notes. A plain-text summary for language models is at /llms.txt.
